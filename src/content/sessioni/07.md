descrizione: "Ficufresche\n\nA musical project born from a deep passion for the women's songs of rural communities, often forgotten by a world that barely notices how much the land can give an awareness of being.\n\n“Some years ago we began recording the voices of peasant women from our land, in the small hamlets of Sessa Aurunca, in the province of Caserta. Elderly women gave us many melodies, many of them marked by the rhythms of work, which through their voices and the lyrics of the oral tradition gave life to simple, bare songs, yet rich in emotions that come from far away, full of harmonies that even today only the land can let us taste.\n\nWe decided to bring these songs back to life to pass them on to others, to make up for the generations that were not able to preserve them, and to remember all the women who, through song, which often began by describing work in the fields, managed to express everything that happened, from simple things like lost loves to ideas that were very useful for facing life.”\n\nStornelli a dispetto/Metti na Fava\n\nA song that speaks, through metaphors, of how unacceptable it is to love two people at the same time.\nThe verses are preceded by a song “a dispetto”, in which people insulted each other, most of the time in a playful, light-hearted way.\n\nImma Argiento\nMaura Sciullo\nLoreta Simeone"
